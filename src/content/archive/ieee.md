## Making things for other people

With Washington University’s IEEE chapter, I worked on mechanical design and manufacturing for a fully autonomous trash-collecting robot intended for the 2020 IEEE robotics competition.

The group also built synchronized light-up dandiya for the university’s Raas team to use during Diwali. Both projects connected CAD and fabrication to a concrete goal: making something another team could actually use.
